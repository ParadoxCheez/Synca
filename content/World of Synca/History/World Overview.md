Hello DNDers, offering a quick summary of the world I am planning to have for our DND that will start at the beginning of next year. 

Every main race has their place of origin. These are the main races: 
- Human
- Aarakocra
- Goblin
- Dwarf
- Dragon/DragonBorn
- Fairy
- Lizardfolk
- Goliaths
- Elves
This does not mean that the other races do not exist but it just means they are not native to the land. However, a large majority of their race may live with another race e.g. Arakocra and Owlin or Dragon and Satyr (These are not fixed, just examples currently). 

Races that are very human in shape e.g. githyanki and genasi originated from humans but years of diversity with other races lead to such evolutions.  Same with races that have some similarities e.g. Orcs are mutated goblins due to lore stuff that you will find out in the future.

Including this there is 3 main cities which are (think of republic city in avatar) cultural hotspots where most of the modern-day-esque stuff occur e.g. the corporations who deal with the dungeons appearing all over the landscape.

3 main cities are called Nebula, Modulo and Endo.

Set in a world where gates (portals) open in places and adventuring parties get paid by the government(s) to deal with the gates that lead to various dungeons 

Outside lore stuff:
All characters must be adult
4d6 Drop the lowest
dnd 5e 2014 ruling (Will go over homebrew stuff eventually)

Any other questions ask me or if you have any advice for me lmk as it is my first time dming.

In the world of Synca, the 8 races (Not the dragon/dragonborn) lived in peace for many years in their respective communities until the timely brothers (chronomancy/chronourgy wizards) told the human race that they need to prepare for the world to change, a time where the proportion of races in the world would change. The leaders of the human race felt threatened by this and started a war with the 7 other races. A vicious war which lead to the birth of many other species due to scientists experimenting on their own races to try and create super soldiers to defeat the other races and gain land. This war was thwarted by a team known as the [[Saviours]] made up of people from the different races that opposed the war and eventually brought peace between the 8 races. 

Now that the war had ended, a lot of warriors who had no reason to fight anymore but still the desire felt like they couldn't waste their skills doing nothing so[[ P.R.O.T.E.C.T]] also known as the adventurer's guild was founded for a way for people to hire heroes to help them with the monsters that roamed around for them to tackle.

However, eventually a large majority of monsters/dragons that roamed the continent of Synca was slain so Protect went into a slump for 100 years,  the age of adventurers and warriors had ended.

In this time science boomed and teleportation gates became the norm for travelling between places and with the discovery of commercial teleportation, from a distant island far away, Amaghị

Until the librarian was came to the world. A woman with the power to take people in out different timelines and stories in order to have them put their adventuring skills to use to save others.

And now we are in the modern day, the 8 races still have their main places they inhabit but we have some cities where they embrace multiculturality: Endo, Modulo and Nebula, the chosen capital city.
