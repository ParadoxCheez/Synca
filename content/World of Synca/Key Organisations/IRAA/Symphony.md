A prominent member of the [[IRAA]]. Symphony is a human who was raised in the [[Place Where Happiness is Born (PWHB)]] and came to Synca, at a young age, hoping to tale the tales of fairies and the music they bring. When he attempted to, he was ridiculed by the people of Synca and called an insane man. Whenever he tried to prove where PWHB was, the queen of the fairies [[Octave]] hid its whereabouts in fear of what [[Base]] would do if they found them. After finding out about the story of [[Titania]], Symphony realised that the authority that was controlling Synca from the shadows was evil and swore to bring them to an end.

Despite all of this, he did not have the resources to enact his plan and was struggling in the cities of [[Endo]]. Until on their first tour of Synca, the 40th leader of [[Amaghị]], [[Emeka]] came through Endo and [[Jagga]] met Symphony and fell in love with his music and became great friends. From then on, he was raised on Amaghị with Jagga as brothers. Not only did they get along so well, but as they got older they grew to share the same ideals and opinions on Base. However, their attitudes to the matter differed vastly and as Jagga grew to be a leading figure in Amaghị, hoping to make it a strong enough political place to make difference, Symphony worked his way up the [[IRAA]] and became one of its "Stars".


Now, Symphony fights for the IRAA combining both music and creation to make art.
