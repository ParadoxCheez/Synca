Remova Kurvic, one of the founding members of the [[Saviours]] was an engineer from the [[Greater Plains]].

Remova was a young engineer who wasn't the biggest fan of the ranger lifestyle instilled into the Elves there. She was never strong enough to draw the bow. It was a life not meant for her. She began studying ways to mechanise the process of shooting bows which lead her to developing the thing that lead to be known as the gun. However, that didn't satisfy her enough and she wanted to make it more powerful, having it only be mechanical was not enough.

In an effort to learn about ways to imbue magic to the mechanical system she had, she began traversing the world to try and find a way to do so. That's when she bumped into [[Max Stevens]] and they formed their bond.

She also has a horse called Moina.
