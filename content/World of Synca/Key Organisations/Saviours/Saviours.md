The group that ended the 7 race war. Comprised of scientists that worked for the 7 races during the war. They all collectively shared that the destruction and death caused by the war had gone too far and they believed that they should use the things they've worked on for peace and not further war.

This group is comprised of:
[[Max Stevens]] - Human
[[Bane Brick]] - Goblin turns into Orc
[[Remova Kurvic]] - Elf with a gun
[[Jamie Mary Meg]] - Half elf that can change sizes
[[Jack Snow]] - Lizardfolk who built armour
[[Bren the Hammer]] - Half Giant with a Hammer

These 5 warriors put aside their differences used what they learnt to force peace between the 7 nations.

100 years ahead, no one knows where they are, most assume dead but no bodies have been found.
