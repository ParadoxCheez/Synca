A very wealthy Elf family that has been very involved in the Elf political scene for many generations. They were one of the 5 seats in the [[Pent]] in the reign of [[Skylar]].  A very important family in Elven history despite their sinister and evil tendencies. Nicknamed "Lauva" by the other 4 seats, meaning "Darkness of Elves" because of their evil history. Their connections to the old dragons of the world of Synca is what allowed to rise to power because having them against the Greater Plains would have been a catastrophe.
