The place where the majority of high elves live in a community that they consider to be highly civilised compared to their counterparts, the elves in [[The wild]]. Lead by [[Crystal]], the elves of Utopia believe that a majority of their problems can be solved through ventures of science that explore non-combative ways. They live by those doctrines and pursue science in the hope of scientific advancements that can protect them rather than training people of physical discipline like the wild lead by her brother, [[Olas]].
