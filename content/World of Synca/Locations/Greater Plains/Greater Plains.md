The greater plains are a large area which is divided in 2 regions.  [[Utopia]] and [[The wild]] . Despite having a smaller territory, the High elves of Utopia believe they are better than the wood elves of the wild as they think they are barbaric continuing to live in the the wild while having the ability to build sophisticated infrastructure. These two nations are not at war but the tides are high. The two leaders had differing ideals to how they should live life. [[Crystal]], the older one, believed in change and growth while [[Olas]] believed they should stay close to their roots in the wilds and live among nature.
