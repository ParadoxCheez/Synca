Modulo is the second biggest city not owned by a one race and also ran by the [[Base]]. This city is much closer to the greater plains so it is populated by mostly elves and races that have more animalistic features. It is the centre of trade in Synca where most merchants go to trade due to the Greater plains being mostly undiscovered and the revolutionary science that the high elves have to offer.
