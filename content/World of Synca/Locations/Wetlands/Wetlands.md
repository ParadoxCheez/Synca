The Wetlands is the place where the Lizardfolk take up home. A large expanse of waters that has had an entire civilisation built on top of it, lead by [[Cypher the Glorious]]. A race who mostly keep to themselves due to their fear and distrust of the outer world. It is rare to see them out of the [[Wetlands]] but you always see at least one in each location.
