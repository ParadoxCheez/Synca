Crystal is the leader of the high elves. At odds with her brother, [[Olas]], Crystal believes that we should constantly grow and change because living by the virtues of the past leaves us there. She despises the ritual and traditions that have been in their culture for generations and chooses to build [[Utopia]] as a city that never stays stagnant. She fears that her brother could keep a better defence of [[The wild]] if he opened his mind to the powers that science has.
