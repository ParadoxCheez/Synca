Most sceptical of the race leaders, Cypher leads the Lizardfolk from the [[Wetlands]]. His distrust has lead to many of the other leaders not making accommodations for Lizardfolk in their settlements (For the sole exception of [[Zod Greyhelm]], of course). Due to their need to hydrate frequently, he has strong distrust for the water outside the Wetlands as when he went on his first visit trip to [[Modulo]] and the water there blinded him with one eye. Despite this, the Lizardfolk have prospered under the 10 years he has lead them but their socialisation with other cultures and races is limited
