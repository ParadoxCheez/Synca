Jagga the 42nd leader of Amaghị is one of the most progressive minds in the world. He has lead the Dragonborn for the past 10 years. He leads a progressive and open mindset as he wishes to lead a race of people who are not constrained by their forefathers. Knowing how Dragonborns get treated outside the Amaghị, he knows he needs to be quick on his wit and strong willed to soothe tensions his ancestors created.
