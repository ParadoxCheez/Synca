The current mayor of [[Nebula]], he is just incredible mayor. When he was younger, he grew up in [[Endo]] and saw how neglect can turn a city to ruin. When he left Endo, he told himself that no matter what happens he won't let that happen to the city that gave him shelter. From then on, he worked as hard possible to make Nebula as beautiful as it is today which lead to him being voted as mayor.
