The Librarian (also known as The)

A mysterious figure who appeared in the world for a reason no one can figure out. She gave purpose to the fighters in the world as they had lost all reason to fight. No one knows anything about him. They exist in some plane that no one knows.

Names herself The Librarian as she believes she holds the stories of the existence.

You can reach The Librarian's domain by placing one of her coins on the floor as it opens a portal to her realm.
